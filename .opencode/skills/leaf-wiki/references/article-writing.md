# Article Writing

## Style

Write like a textbook chapter, not a note. Every article should answer: what is this, why does it matter, how does it connect to what we already know.

## Structure

1. Opening sentence states the claim or concept directly.
2. Context — where this fits in the project's architecture or domain.
3. Detail — the substance, with specifics.
4. Connections — `[[links]]` to related articles.
5. Gaps — anything uncertain or unexplored, flagged explicitly.

## Rules

- Cite raw sources by id in the `sources` frontmatter field.
- Never copy raw text into articles. Synthesize.
- If a claim can't be sourced, mark confidence `low` and say why.
- Prefer one focused article over one sprawling one. Chunk aggressively.
- Dual links: `[[slug]]` plus normal markdown where it reads better.
